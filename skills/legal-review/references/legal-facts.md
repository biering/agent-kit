# Current legal facts (verified 2026-08; re-check if stale)

- **§ 5 DDG** (Digitale-Dienste-Gesetz) replaced **§ 5 TMG** on **14 May 2024**. Imprint content
  is unchanged; a "§ 5 TMG" reference is a warning risk.
- **TDDDG** (Telekommunikation-Digitale-Dienste-Datenschutz-Gesetz) replaced **TTDSG** on
  **14 May 2024**. § 25 (cookie consent) is unchanged in substance; update the wording. § 25
  violations: fines up to **€300,000**.
- **EU ODR platform** (`ec.europa.eu/consumers/odr`) was **permanently shut down 20 July 2025**
  (Regulation (EU) 2024/3228). Any imprint or AGB link to it must be **removed**.
- **Google Consent Mode v2** is required (since March 2024) for EEA Google Ads/Analytics;
  without it Google throttles EEA conversion modelling.
- **EU-US Data Privacy Framework (DPF)**: the adequacy decision (July 2023) covers *certified*
  US vendors; otherwise SCCs + TIA.

Sources: IHK, e-recht24 and it-recht-kanzlei on DDG vs. TMG and TDDDG vs. TTDSG; WBS.legal and
mmv-recht on the 20 July 2025 ODR shutdown. The legal landscape shifts, so re-verify dates before
relying on them, and update the date above when you do.
