# Unslop scoring rubric

Eight criteria, 1–5 each; a rewrite passes at **32/40**. Adapted for DE/EN from
theclaymethod/unslop's rubric (MIT). Score the *rewritten* text; on an
audit-only pass you can score the original to give the user a number.

| # | Criterion | 5 looks like | 1 looks like |
|---|-----------|--------------|--------------|
| 1 | **Directness** | Opens with the point; every sentence advances it | Throat-clearing, hedge-stacked, point buried |
| 2 | **Rhythm** | Sentence lengths vary naturally (read it aloud) | Uniform 15–20-word sentences, templated cadence |
| 3 | **Concrete verbs** | build, cut, ship, rechnen, zeigen | leverage, utilize, facilitate, ermöglichen, gewährleisten everywhere |
| 4 | **Reader trust** | Assumes competence, no "in other words" | "Let that sink in", over-explaining, patronizing |
| 5 | **Authenticity** | A specific person with opinions wrote this | Either obvious AI tells or clean-but-soulless corporate neutral |
| 6 | **Density** | Every word necessary | Filler frames, adverb padding |
| 7 | **Fact preservation** | Every number, name, date, URL, hedge intact | Any fact changed or dropped = max 2 here, and the rewrite fails outright |
| 8 | **Template avoidance** | Organic structure | Rule-of-three, binary contrasts, preview/recap, punch endings |

Scoring guide: 36+ excellent, 32–35 passing, 28–31 needs another pass,
below 28 rewrite again from the original (not from your own failed rewrite —
iterating on a bad rewrite compounds drift from the source facts).

Criterion 5 cuts both ways on purpose: stripping every tell but also every
opinion, contraction, and first-person produces text that scores 3, not 5.
The goal is a human voice, not a sterilized one.
