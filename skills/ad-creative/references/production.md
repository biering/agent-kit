# Production

How the brief becomes an asset that survives a feed: the first second, the sound-off viewer,
and the sizes and text limits of each placement. Specs move; every number below has its source
and the date it was checked. A number older than three months is rechecked on the platform's own
page before you rely on it, and fixed here when it changed.

## The first second

- **The first frame is the visual hook** (element 4). It is a frame, not a fade-in, a logo or a
  title card. A viewer decides in the first second whether there is a reason to stay.
- **The copy hook is on screen in the first second** and spoken in the first three. About eight
  spoken words fit three seconds at a normal pace (`unslop`'s platform limits, when present).
- **Show the product's real screen.** For software the most stopping image is usually the
  reader's own trouble on a screen they recognise. Every screen in the ad is the product
  actually running (`app-screenshots` holds the same rule for the store).
- **No brand first.** The logo and the product name come after the hope sale, or at the end.
- **One idea per shot,** cut on the sentence, never on the beat alone.

## Sound off

Make the ad work with the sound off, because most placements autoplay muted and the viewer
decides before unmuting. Meta calls captions "optional but recommended"; TikTok's own research
says its viewers watch with sound on, so on TikTok write for both. No official current figure
for the share of muted viewing was found; do not quote one.

- **Captions burned in,** not only uploaded as a file: every spoken word, in the brand's font,
  inside the safe zone, two lines at most, high contrast.
- **The story reads without the voice.** Mute the cut and watch it once; if the mechanism is
  lost, the screen has to say it.
- **Music is licensed for ads,** from the platform's commercial library or a licence on file.
  A trending track from organic posts is not licensed for ads.

## Sizes, lengths and text limits

Checked 2026-11-20. "Unverified" means the platform's own page could not be read and the value
comes from a secondary source: check it in a browser before launch.

### Meta (Facebook, Instagram)

| Field | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Feed image | 4:5 recommended, 1440 x 1800, JPG or PNG, 30 MB max; Instagram feed accepts 1.91:1 to 4:5 | [FB feed](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/image/facebook-feed), [IG feed](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/image/instagram-feed) |
| Stories image | 9:16, 1440 x 2560 | [IG story](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/image/instagram-story) |
| Feed video | 4:5, 1440 x 1800; Facebook 1 s to 241 min, Instagram 1 s to 60 min; 4 GB | [video](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/video), [IG feed video](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/video/instagram-feed) |
| Reels video | 9:16, 1440 x 2560, up to 15 min, 4 GB | [IG Reels](https://www.facebook.com/business/ads-guide/update/video/instagram-reels) |
| Reels and Stories safe zone | keep text off the top 14 %, the bottom 35 % and 6 % each side (on 1080 x 1920: about 270 px top, 670 px bottom, 65 px sides) | Reels and Story pages above |
| Primary text | Facebook 50 to 150 characters; Instagram feed and Stories 125; Instagram Reels 44 | FB feed, IG feed, IG Reels pages above |
| Headline | Facebook 27 characters, Instagram 40 | FB feed, IG feed pages above |
| Description | not on the current pages; unverified | none |

Facebook Reels length is unverified (the page returned 404). The guide shows Awareness-objective
specs; other objectives may differ.

### Google search and display

| Field | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Responsive search ad | headlines 30 characters (3 to 15), descriptions 90 (2 to 4), two paths of 15 | [RSA](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7684791) |
| Display image | 1.91:1 at 1200 x 628, 1:1 at 1200 x 1200, 9:16 at 900 x 1600; 5120 KB | [responsive display](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/9823397) |
| Display description | 80 characters | same |

Every headline of a responsive search ad must make sense next to any other: Google combines them.
Pin only what the law needs in a fixed place.

### Apple Search Ads

The advertiser writes no ad text. The default ad is built from the product page; a variation
picks one custom product page made in App Store Connect
([ad variations](https://ads.apple.com/app-store/help/ads/0077-create-ad-variations)). The
creative work is the custom product page, made with `app-screenshots` and `aso`.

## Before export

- [ ] Each size the placement needs, rendered from one master, the safe zone checked on each.
- [ ] Captions burned in and spell-checked; the ad watched once muted.
- [ ] Every text field within its limit, the point before the fold.
- [ ] The file named after its variant (`<angle>_<format>_<variable>_<v>`), the same string as
      its `utm_content`.
