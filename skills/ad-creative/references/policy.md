# Policy: law and platform rules before launch

What an ad for a small software product sold in the EU may not do, with the rule behind each
line. This is a checklist for the writer, not legal advice; a claim that is close to a line goes
to the `legal-review` skill and, when it matters, to a lawyer. Checked 2026-11-20. Items marked
*unverified* could not be read on the official page that day; check them in a browser before
relying on them.

## EU and German law

The EU rules are the Unfair Commercial Practices Directive (UCPD, 2005/29/EC, as amended by
2019/2161); Germany implements them in the UWG, whose blacklist (Anhang zu § 3 Abs. 3) uses the
same numbers as UCPD Annex I. A blacklisted practice is unlawful in every case, with no weighing
of whether anyone was misled.

| Rule | Source | For the ad writer |
|---|---|---|
| Misleading actions | UCPD Art. 6; [UWG § 5](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__5.html) | every claim about what it does, what it costs and what it achieves is true and provable |
| Misleading omissions | UCPD Art. 7; [UWG § 5a](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__5a.html) | leaving out the subscription, the tier, the platform it needs is misleading too |
| Essential information | [UWG § 5b](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/__5b.html) | an offer with a price names total price and trader; shown reviews need a statement of how they are checked (Abs. 3) |
| Limited time, falsely | [UWG Anhang](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/uwg_2004/anhang.html) Nr. 7, UCPD Annex I No. 7 | no "only today", countdown or "ends soon" that is not true |
| Bait offers | UWG Anhang Nr. 5 and 6 | no price we cannot or will not deliver, no switch to another product |
| Hidden advertising | UWG Anhang Nr. 11 | an advertorial or a fake organic post is marked as an ad |
| Cures | UWG Anhang Nr. 17 | never claim it cures or relieves an illness |
| "Free" | UWG Anhang Nr. 20 | "free" only when it costs nothing; a trial that turns into a subscription says so |
| Reviews | UWG Anhang Nr. 23b and 23c | no fake reviews or endorsements, no misrepresented real ones, no "verified buyers" without checking |
| Price reductions | [PAngV § 11](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/pangv_2022/__11.html), Dir. 98/6/EC Art. 6a | "was 29, now 19" or "-30 %" is measured against the lowest price of the 30 days before the reduction (CJEU C-330/23, 2024-09-26, [press release](https://curia.europa.eu/jcms/upload/docs/application/pdf/2024-09/cp240152en.pdf)) |
| Health claims | [HWG § 1](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/heilmwerbg/__1.html), [§ 3](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/heilmwerbg/__3.html); MDR (EU) 2017/745 Art. 2(1) | a claim about recognising, removing or relieving a disease brings the ad under the HWG, and software with a medical purpose is a medical device: never claim one |
| Ads are identifiable | DSA (EU) 2022/2065 Art. 26 *(unverified text)* | the platform labels the ad; never dress it as news or a user's post |

UCPD and DSA texts on EUR-Lex could not be fetched on the check date; the article numbers match
the German texts, which were read.

## The checklist

Each line maps to a launch check in the brief ([templates/brief.md](../templates/brief.md)).

1. **Facts.** Every fact in the creative is a premise with a source, re-read on launch day.
2. **Urgency.** Only a real, sourced end date. No stock (software has none), no timer, no
   "last chance". After the date, the ad stops or changes.
3. **Proof.** No invented number, review, rating, testimonial or result. A real quote needs the
   author's consent; an example figure says it is an example.
4. **Third parties.** No real character, game, film, celebrity, creator or brand, no lookalike,
   no "in the style of". A competitor only in a comparison the repo doc allows, with sourced
   facts.
5. **Bait.** No sexualised bait, no shock image unrelated to the product.
6. **Health.** No medical or health claim, for any app.
7. **Personal attributes.** The copy describes a situation, never asserts the reader's
   condition.
8. **Price.** A reduction is shown against the lowest price of the 30 days before it; "free"
   is free; a subscription says it is one.
9. **Platform.** The placement's own policy pages above, read for this ad.
10. **Landing page.** It repeats the ad's promise and holds the same facts (`ads-landingpage`).
